# Privacy Policy - Goal Tracker App

**Last Updated:** December 6, 2025

## Introduction
Goal Tracker App ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and share information when you use our goal tracking application.

## Information We Collect

### Account Information
- **Google Account Data**: Name, email address, and profile photo when you sign in with Google
- **User-Generated Content**: Goals you create, descriptions, categories, completion status, and privacy settings
- **Usage Data**: Streaks, points earned, reactions, and interactions with other users' content

### Automatically Collected Information
- **Device Information**: Browser type, operating system, IP address
- **Usage Analytics**: Pages visited, features used, time spent in app
- **Cookies**: We use cookies to maintain your session and remember preferences

## How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to:
- Provide and maintain the goal tracking service
- Authenticate your account and ensure security
- Display your profile information to other users as per your privacy settings
- Calculate and display statistics (streaks, points, progress)
- Improve our services and develop new features
- Communicate with you about updates and support

## Third-Party Services

### Google Firebase
We use Google Firebase for:
- Authentication (Google Sign-In)
- Database storage (Firestore)
- Hosting services

Firebase's privacy policy: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

### Google AdSense (Advertising)
We display advertisements using Google AdSense. Google may collect:
- Cookie data and device identifiers
- IP address and location data
- Browsing behavior for ad personalization

**For users under 13**: We use non-personalized ads only and comply with COPPA regulations.

You can opt out of personalized advertising at: https://www.google.com/settings/ads

### Analytics
We may use analytics services to understand how users interact with our app. These services may collect anonymous usage data.

## Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Our service may be used by children under 13 with parental consent. We:
- Do NOT collect personal information beyond what's necessary for the service
- Use only non-personalized, family-safe ads for all users
- Do NOT share children's data with third parties except as required for service operation
- Allow parents to review and delete their child's information upon request

**Parental Rights**: Parents can email us to:
- Review their child's information
- Request deletion of their child's account
- Refuse further collection of their child's data

## Data Sharing

We DO NOT sell your personal information. We may share data only:
- **With Your Consent**: When you choose to make goals public
- **For Legal Reasons**: If required by law or to protect rights and safety
- **Service Providers**: Firebase, AdSense (as described above)
